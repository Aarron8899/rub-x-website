Why Support Rub-X?

Rub-X is a breakthrough, field-tested green automation system for rubber plantations. It replaces unsafe manual labor with a reliable, low-cost, energy-efficient solution — aligned with multiple UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Yet despite repeated calls for support, no institution has offered to host or validate the system. Rub-X is at risk of being lost or misappropriated. We are calling on all global citizens to sign in support of institutional hosting under the United Nations framework.
