Rub-X Neutrality and Independence Statement

Rub-X is a citizen-led innovation with no affiliation to any government, political entity, or commercial interest. It is an independent initiative developed solely by inventor Hanmin Hu, based on global sustainability needs and technological feasibility.

Non-Governmental, Non-State Controlled

The Rub-X system was conceived, developed, and patented by Hanmin Hu, a China-born US permanent resident. The project has not received any financial, institutional, or policy support from the governments of China, the United States, or any other state.

All intellectual property is held personally or through Hanmin LLC (USA). The project is committed to transparency and global benefit. No government exerts control over the Rub-X system.

Call for Responsible Hosting

Rub-X is a proven, field-tested green technology designed to replace hazardous labor in rubber agriculture. We have approached more than 20 international institutions, seeking responsible hosting and global deployment partnerships.

Our neutrality ensures Rub-X can serve all regions and communities equally, without political or national bias. We call on multilateral institutions, especially under the United Nations system, to respond with meaningful support rather than silence.

Warning Against Misappropriation

In the absence of institutional cooperation, Rub-X reserves the right to seal its technology while maintaining full intellectual property claims. Any unauthorized replication or adoption of the system, especially by institutions previously contacted, will be considered a violation of ethical and legal norms.

Silencing a solution does not erase the need it was designed to address. To deny this technology is to prolong unsafe, unsustainable practices.
